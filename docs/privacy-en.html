Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Ink & Verse is operated by an independent developer. This policy explains how the App handles information.

1. The English reading experience

When your device or the App is set to English, Ink & Verse is a reading app. Poems, translations, commentary, poet profiles, and guqin music are built into the App. Reading, searching, and saving poems all happen on your device, and the App does not send your reading activity, searches, or saved poems anywhere.

Saved poems and settings. Your saved poems and display preferences, such as typeface and script, are stored locally on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers, and we cannot access them.

Purchases. Ink & Verse Pro is purchased through Apple. Apple handles payment, and we never receive your payment details. To confirm and restore your Pro access, the App uses RevenueCat to manage purchases. RevenueCat assigns the App a random, anonymous user ID and stores the purchase records linked to it (items purchased, purchase and expiration dates, and transaction receipts), along with basic technical information such as app version, OS version, and region. This anonymous ID does not include your name, Apple ID, or contact details.

Device information. Apart from the anonymous user ID used for purchases, the App does not collect device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or other device information, and it does not track you across apps.

2. Features available only in Chinese

When the App is set to Chinese, it also offers offline poem composition. Those features handle information as follows:

  • Local composition. Mood choices, imagery matching, candidate-line selection, and poem assembly are processed on your device and are not sent to an external content-generation service.
  • Location. With your explicit permission, the App uses only your city name for a poem’s inscription. It does not record precise coordinates or store location history.
  • Creative content. Poems you create and seal names are stored locally on your device.

None of these features run while the App is set to English.

3. Storage and security

Your saved poems, settings, and creations stay on your device. We do not operate a user-data server. Purchase records are stored and protected by Apple and RevenueCat under their own privacy policies. Deleting the App permanently deletes its local data.

4. Third-party services

  • Apple App Store. Processes in-app purchases and payment. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat. Verifies purchases and manages Pro access and subscription status. See RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy.

The App does not include advertising, analytics, or social-media SDKs.

5. Children’s privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the revised version here and update the “Last updated” date.
