Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 8, 2026

Woven Verse is operated by an independent developer. This policy explains how the App handles information.

1. Information we handle

Location. With your explicit permission, the App uses only your city name to add a place to a poem’s inscription. It does not record precise coordinates or store location history. You can turn off location access at any time without affecting core features.

Creative content. Your selected imagery, generated lines, saved poems, and seal names are stored locally on your device through UserDefaults. They are not uploaded to our servers, and we cannot access them.

AI requests. To generate candidate lines, imagery options, or AI commentary that you request, the App sends the relevant mood keywords, imagery descriptions, poem text, and author information to Alibaba Cloud Bailian / Tongyi Qianwen. Requests contain only the text needed for that feature and do not include your name, location, or other direct identifiers.

Device information. The App does not actively collect device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or other device information.

2. How information is used

  • Your city name is used only in a poem’s inscription.
  • Imagery keywords are sent to the AI service to generate candidate lines.
  • Poetry search terms are sent to the open poetry library to return results.

3. Storage and security

Personal settings and creative content remain on your device. We do not operate a user-data server. Deleting the App permanently deletes its local data.

4. Third-party services

  • Alibaba Cloud Bailian (Tongyi Qianwen). Used for AI line generation and AI commentary requested by you. See the Alibaba Cloud Privacy Policy.
  • Poetry Spring open poetry library. When you search for poetry that is not built into the App, this service returns the title, author, dynasty, form, and original text. Your search terms are sent to this service.

The App does not include advertising, analytics, or social-media SDKs.

5. Children’s privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the revised version here and update the “Last updated” date. Please review this page periodically.
